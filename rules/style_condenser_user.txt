Compatibility note:
- Keep exactly these fields because the runner structurally requires the older core contract fields.
- Do not add extra arrays such as separate negative_medium_terms, negative_style_terms, or parser copies.

Compact output limits:
- Summaries and anchor_usage_rule: max 16 words each.
- required_style_prefix: max 10 words.
- Each array max 5 items; optional_anchor_pool max 7.
- Each array item max 6 words.
- No explanations, no copied paragraphs, no examples, no duplicate meaning.

Universal source rules:
- Every non-empty field must be grounded in PARSED SOURCE FACTS or RAW VIDEO STYLE.
- Do not invent unrelated medium, genre, era, world, species, technology, setting, palette, character, or plot.
- style_must_not contains only explicit source negatives plus pipeline safety: current lyrics remain primary; no idle/camera-only clips.
- Never put a required positive medium/technique/style into style_must_not. If the source itself conflicts, preserve the positive source term and mention the conflict compactly in style_must_not only if it is explicit.
- No project-default bans: only source-grounded negatives belong in style_must_not.

Style contract rules:
- required_style_prefix is a short copyable prompt prefix from positive source medium/technique/look.
- visual_style_summary summarizes medium/look/palette, not narrative plot.
- allowed_style_elements are desired source motifs/styles, not required every-clip content.

Identity anchor demotion:
- required_recurring_anchors are broad invariants only: overall identity/world class, subject/body class, tone class, or universal design lens.
- Do NOT place named characters, factions, iconic landmarks, seats, monuments, vehicles, weapons, tools, relics, symbols, rituals, destinations, or one-off locations in required_recurring_anchors unless the source explicitly says they appear in every clip.
- Concrete motifs go into optional_anchor_pool, character_or_faction_anchors, location_anchors, or prop_or_symbol_anchors.
- style_continuity_rules must stay abstract: rendering consistency, palette family, tone, body/design logic, broad world class, atmosphere.
- If unsure, demote the anchor. Under-forcing is safer than over-forcing.
- anchor_usage_rule must say current lyrics choose main subject/action/setting; anchors are optional support unless lyrics mention or imply them.
