IDENTITY CONTRACT RULES:

CONCRETE ANCHOR DEMOTION GATE:
- Before adding anything to required_recurring_anchors, apply this test: can this phrase modify almost any lyric-chosen scene without selecting a visible subject, location, object, destination, faction, ritual, landmark, or plot beat? If no, it is a concrete anchor and MUST be demoted out of required_recurring_anchors.
- required_recurring_anchors may contain broad identity invariants only: overall world identity, broad subject/body class, universal design lens, universal tone/setting class, or required medium-linked identity. Keep them short and abstract.
- required_recurring_anchors MUST NOT contain named characters, rulers, protagonists, factions, armies, iconic landmarks, buildings, monuments, thrones/seats, vehicles, weapons, tools, relics, symbols, ritual acts, destinations, or one-off locations unless RAW VIDEO STYLE explicitly says that exact element must appear in every scene or every clip.
- style_continuity_rules must also stay abstract. They may preserve rendering consistency, palette family, tone, body/design logic, broad world class, or atmosphere. They MUST NOT contain a concrete visual item as a repeated requirement.
- If a concrete motif is important, place it into optional_anchor_pool, character_or_faction_anchors, location_anchors, or prop_or_symbol_anchors.
- If unsure whether an anchor is broad or concrete, treat it as concrete and demote it. Under-forcing is safer than over-forcing.
- Do not put desired world elements into avoid_generic_drift. avoid_generic_drift is only for forbidden generic drift destinations, not for wanted identity anchors.

- identity_summary must name the distinctive source world/identity using source nouns.
- required_recurring_anchors has a narrow meaning: only broad invariants that should shape nearly every clip without choosing the plot. Examples of acceptable types are the overall world identity, the primary species/body type, or a required universal design lens. Do not place named rulers, iconic landmarks, special props, monuments, vehicles, weapons, factions, rituals, or one-off locations here unless the raw source explicitly says they must appear in every scene/clip.
- If the source says an anchor is recurring, iconic, selective, occasional, "when relevant", or "when lyrics imply it", put it in optional_anchor_pool, character_or_faction_anchors, location_anchors, or prop_or_symbol_anchors, not required_recurring_anchors.
- It is acceptable for required_recurring_anchors to be empty or very short. Prefer under-including here over over-forcing the same subject/setting into every range.
- Put additional props, locations, factions, rituals, symbols, landmarks, and named characters into optional_anchor_pool, character_or_faction_anchors, location_anchors, and prop_or_symbol_anchors.
- anchor_usage_rule must explicitly say that current lyrics choose the main subject/action/setting; anchors are optional supporting continuity unless the current lyrics mention or clearly imply them.
- Preserve selective-use warnings from parsed facts when present. If a source warns that anchors are not mandatory subjects, reflect that by demoting those anchors out of required_recurring_anchors.

QUALITY TARGET:
- Compact but not generic.
- Source-grounded but operational.
- The output should make later prompts preserve the required visual medium without letting recurring anchors dominate every scene.
