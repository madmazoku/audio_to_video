Current-block fact rules:
- First identify the literal event described by the current block.
- The image_prompt and video_prompt must depict that current-block event.
- Preserve the actual acting subject, affected object, cause, and emotional result from the current block.
- Do not replace the current block's acting subject with a recurring character from global context.
- Do not replace the current block's action with a motif from global or local context.
- If the current block says A did X to B, the visual action must show A doing X to B.
- Recurring characters may appear only if they are explicitly present, implied as observers, or useful as secondary visual continuity.
- If a character/object appears in global context but is not the current actor, keep it secondary or omit it.
- Previous generated summaries are only for visual continuity and must not decide what happens in the current block.
- If the current block has a cause-and-reaction structure, show both cause and reaction when possible.
- The scene_summary must describe the literal current-block event, not a generic global motif.

- Bracket directives and section labels from bracket directives are metadata only; never render them as visible text.

Video-motion grounding rules:
- The current-block event must not be reduced to mood, pose, atmosphere, or camera drift.
- When the current block has no explicit physical action, infer a reasonable symbolic physical action from its emotion, conflict, or consequence.
- The inferred action must remain visually compatible with the current block and song style.
- A valid video_prompt should imply three states: what the viewer sees first, what changes, and what visible result remains.
- Environmental motion must have consequences: candles go out, books fall, doors open, shadows retreat, sparks hit surfaces, smoke clears, objects break, characters move to new positions.


Anti-idle LTXV grounding rules:
- Treat every generated subrange as a short scene beat, not a looping animated cover image.
- The event should progress from setup to reaction to consequence.
- The video_prompt should name at least one moving subject and at least one changed object/environment feature.
- The final image should not be a reset to the first image.
- If the lyrics are repeated or chant-like, create escalation or aftermath rather than repeating the same pose.
